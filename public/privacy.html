Privacy Policy — Guzel Radio

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Guzel Radio ("the app") is a free internet radio player for Android and Wear OS. This page explains what data the app accesses and why.

Data we collect

Guzel Radio does not require an account, and does not collect or store any personal information, contacts, location, or device identifiers.

The app does send the following non-personal technical data while in use:

  • Station stream requests — sent directly to the internet radio station you choose to play, and to the public RadioBrowser directory to list stations. These requests include only the standard information any network request carries (such as IP address), handled by those third-party services.
  • Anonymous stream health pings — when a station plays successfully or fails to load, the app reports the station's ID (not any user identifier) to our health-tracking API at radio.recepguzel.com, so we can surface reliable stations to all users. No account or device identifier is attached to these reports.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite stations are saved only in local app storage (SharedPreferences) on your device. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions used

  • Internet — to stream radio and fetch station lists.
  • Foreground service / media playback — to keep audio playing in the background and show playback controls on the lock screen, notification shade, and Android Auto.
  • Notifications — to show the now-playing media notification required for background playback.

Third parties

The app streams audio directly from the internet radio stations you select, and uses the public RadioBrowser API to list stations. We do not control the privacy practices of individual radio stations or of RadioBrowser; please refer to their own policies if you have concerns.

Children's privacy

Guzel Radio does not knowingly collect data from children and contains no features directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
