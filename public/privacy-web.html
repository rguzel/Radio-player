Privacy Policy — Guzel Radio Web Player

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This page covers the Guzel Radio web player at radio.recepguzel.com.

Using the Android or Wear OS app? This page does not apply to it. The Android app does not collect IP addresses or location, and does not take part in the play or country statistics described here. See the Android app privacy policy.

No account, no tracking

The web player needs no account and uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Your favorite stations are saved only in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us.

Play statistics and listener countries

When you start a station, the web player reports the station's ID to our server. We use this for the "Most Played" and "Listeners" statistics:

  • Station counts: how often each station was played.
  • Country counts: how many plays came from each country. The country is determined from your IP address by our network provider, Cloudflare, and passed to us as a two-letter country code. We do not determine your location ourselves, and we never learn anything more precise than the country.

We store only these totals per station and per country. Your IP address is held briefly in memory (up to 30 minutes) so the same play isn't counted twice, and is never saved with the statistics.

Server logs

Like most websites, our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page and browser type) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are deleted automatically after 14 days and are not used for statistics.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare delivers this website and sees your connection, as described in its privacy policy.
  • Radio stations: audio streams directly from the station you choose (or, for stations without HTTPS, through our server, which passes the audio on without recording it). Station logos load from the address each station lists in RadioBrowser.
  • RadioBrowser: station lists, and anonymous clicks and votes that help rank popular stations.

We do not control the privacy practices of radio stations or RadioBrowser.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
